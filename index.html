00 — PAPER ABSTRACT

Abstract

This study asks whether historical search-performance and content signals can identify existing pages that should be prioritized for refresh review. The analysis uses the bundled anonymized FlyRank starter dataset containing 30,000 content-page rows across pseudonymized clients. The target is is_declining_label = trend_direction == "down", while label-derived trend fields and grouping identifiers are excluded from the model feature matrix. Logistic regression, decision tree, and random forest models are compared with a transparent ranked baseline using a client-level holdout and fixed random seed 42. The random forest achieved 0.750 ROC AUC and 0.740 Precision@50 versus 0.627 ROC AUC and 0.240 Precision@50 for the baseline. The output is a ranked refresh-review queue intended to help editors inspect higher-priority pages first; it is decision support, not an automatic publishing or SEO-action system.

01 — PROBLEM FRAMING

The Decision This Supports

UNIT OF ANALYSIS

Individual content page

The system operates at page level and ranks pages for editorial review when review capacity is limited.

OUTPUT

Risk score + ranked queue

The pipeline produces a model probability, rank, confidence category, reason codes, and suggested editorial action.

HUMAN ACTION

Review before changing

An editor verifies the page, search context, performance signals, and reason codes before deciding whether to refresh or monitor.

COST OF ERROR

False positive vs false negative

False positives consume editorial capacity. False negatives can leave declining pages unattended.

The strongest measured signals are consistency of impressions, recent impression volume, average position, and content age. Content length contributes, but ranks below several exposure, position, and age signals.

Feature importance is predictive association within this model. It does not establish that changing a feature will cause search traffic to change.

07 — RECOMMENDATION

Turn the Score into an Editorial Workflow

01

High-confidence review

Start with the highest-ranked pages. Verify page context, search intent, performance signals, and reason codes before editing.

02

CTR / engagement investigation

Use refresh_and_review_ctr and refresh_and_review_engagement as investigation prompts, not automatic prescriptions.

03

Refresh

Use refresh recommendations as a manual review starting point where decline and visibility signals overlap.

04

Expand and refresh

Use expand_and_refresh cautiously; word count alone is not evidence that expansion will improve performance.

05

Monitor

Reserve capacity for stronger candidates and keep lower-priority pages under observation.

LIMITS

Decision support, not causal proof

  • No root-cause diagnosis.
  • No automatic deletion, redirect, rewrite, or publishing action.
  • Evaluation is based on the 30,000-row anonymized starter slice.
  • New-content behavior is not established; the preparation pipeline filters to pages at least 90 days old.
